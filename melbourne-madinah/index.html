About Melbourne Madinah

A modern Madinah, inspired by a timeless model.

We like to call it the Madinah, in memory of the holy city of our Prophet Muhammad ﷺ. In the midst of the hustle and bustle of the CBD, it's a retreat to take a break from the world — a place to meet friends, make friends, connect socially and spiritually, and above all grow in our love for Allah.

This is a house of Allah — not a centre run by us. So everyone is welcome. We don't judge, nor do we impose: hijabi or not, bearded or not, long pants or running shorts. Whoever you are, if you feel an inclination to learn, to practise, or simply to pray, you'll feel at home here.

Beyond the prayer areas there are lounge spaces to relax and unwind — table-tennis and pool tables, couches and coffee tables to share food around. Melbourne Madinah is an initiative of Mercy Mission, delivered with partners who serve the city's Muslims every day.

Our story so far
  1. 2011

    The Madinah is born. $380,000 is raised at the Australian Annual Islamic Conference, and the first home opens at 36 La Trobe Street in the blessed month of Ramadan.

  2. 2013

    A second home. The Madinah moves to 47 City Road, Southbank — and, by Allah's will, doesn't miss a single Jumu'ah, completing the move within a week.

  3. Today

    In the heart of the city. The Madinah's home at 359 Exhibition Street welcomes the community for prayer, classes and gatherings — right in the Melbourne CBD.

Programs, classes & gatherings

More than a prayer room.

Our education program runs from basic to advanced — primarily Arabic, Tajweed and Islamic Studies — alongside structured, term-based Sunday classes for kids that are loved by families across Melbourne. Throughout the week the Madinah fills with learning and community life.

Youth · Ages 12–16

Grounded

A six-week program raising confident, grounded and socially aware young Muslims — identity, deeds, technology and responsibility, with mentors, workshops and excursions.

Sundays · 10:00am–12:30pm

Evening gatherings

Madinah Nights

Evenings of reflection, reminders and community in the heart of the city — a chance to slow down, reconnect and find stillness amid the week.

Qur'an & Tajweed Classes

Journey with the Qur'an — our annual intensive memorisation program, open to all, brothers & sisters. Plus Tajweed for adults — mastering the rules of beautiful recitation — and Arabic & Islamic Studies.
