# Short-form video script (Reel / YouTube Short / TikTok — ~30-40 sec)

**Topic:** "Your business is losing leads while you sleep" — promotes Al-Falah Marketing's AI & Automation service.
**Format:** Talking-head or voiceover-over-B-roll, on-screen captions throughout (sound-off viewing is common).
**Branding:** Keep a small "Al-Falah Marketing" logo/wordmark watermark in a corner for the full duration (standard for repost/reshare identification), in addition to the full CTA card at the end.

---

**[0:00–0:04] HOOK — on screen, bold text over a phone showing a WhatsApp inbox with unread messages**
> VO/Caption: "Your customers are messaging you right now. Are you answering?"

**[0:04–0:12] PROBLEM — cut to someone visibly busy/away from their phone**
> VO: "Every hour you're not online, someone's asking about your prices, your services, your availability — and if they don't hear back fast, they message your competitor instead."
> Caption: "Missed messages = missed money."

**[0:12–0:22] SOLUTION — cut to a clean graphic/screen recording of an automated chat reply**
> VO: "This is what automation actually does. It answers instantly, qualifies the lead, and books the call — so by the time you check your phone, it's already handled."
> Caption: "Instant replies. Qualified leads. Booked calls."

**[0:22–0:30] PROOF — on-screen stat card**
> VO: "One of our clients saw inquiries jump 300% after we set this up for them."
> Caption: "+300% inquiries — real client result."

**[0:30–0:38] CTA — logo/end card**
> VO: "Al-Falah Marketing builds this for businesses across Guinea and Sierra Leone. Message us — link in bio."
> Caption: "DM us to see what this looks like for your business. 📲"

---

## Production notes
- Keep captions on-screen the whole time — most viewers watch muted.
- Use the brand's primary blue (`#3B82F6`) and accent amber (`#F59E0B`) for on-screen text/cards to stay on-brand.
- If filming a real talking-head version, French and English cuts are both worth doing given the bilingual audience — same script, dubbed/re-recorded.
