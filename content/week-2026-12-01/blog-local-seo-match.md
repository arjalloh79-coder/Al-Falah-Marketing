# Why your Google Business Profile and website must match

**Al-Falah Marketing · 1 Dec 2026 · 4 min**

A restaurant in Coyah, a clinic in Freetown, and a home-service company in Laurel all lose customers the same way: the details on their Google listing do not match the website.

Customers open the map, see a business, then tap the website to check. If the name is slightly different, the hours conflict, or the phone number is missing on one side, they leave. In 2026 this also affects how AI search summaries describe the business.

## What “matching” means

The business name, address, phone, and hours are identical. The services listed on the Google Business Profile appear on the website in the same language the customer is using. Photos on both places are real shots of the shop or the work, not stock images. The website loads quickly on a phone and makes the next step obvious.

When these pieces line up, Google has clearer signals. Customers have clearer trust.

## Why it matters in these markets

In Guinea and Sierra Leone many searches happen on mobile data. A mismatched listing wastes the click. In Maryland the same habit exists: people compare two or three options in under a minute and pick the one that looks consistent.

Local SEO still drives calls and messages. Generative results (AI overviews and conversational answers) pull from the same sources. Consistent information raises the chance you are mentioned accurately.

## How Al-Falah approaches it

We start with the real business details and the languages customers use. We set up or clean the Google Business Profile, then build or update the website so the two stay in sync. Branding (logo, colors, tone) stays the same across both. Simple automation can later help with review replies or FAQ updates, but the foundation is accurate, matching information.
